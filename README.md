## GlobalTrade Hub Ecomarce Marketplace

![GlobalTrade Hub](./demo/GlobalTrade%20Hub.png)

GlobalTrade Hub is a digital marketplace that connects buyers and sellers from around the world. It provides a platform for businesses to trade goods and services, and for individuals to purchase products from various countries.

## Features:
- **Multi-language support**: GlobalTrade Hub supports multiple languages, making it accessible to a broader audience .
- **Secure payment processing**: The platform ensures secure payment processing, protecting both buyers and sellers from potential
fraud.
- **Real-time inventory management**: Sellers can manage their inventory in real-time, ensuring accurate stock levels
and reducing the risk of overselling.
- **Rating and review system**: Buyers can leave ratings and reviews for sellers, helping to build trust
and credibility within the community.
- **Multi-currency support**: GlobalTrade Hub supports multiple currencies, making it easier for buyers and sellers to conduct 
transactions across different regions.
- **24/7 customer support**: The platform offers 24/7 customer support, ensuring that
any issues or concerns are addressed promptly.
